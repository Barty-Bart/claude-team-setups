# Explainer video brief: "Why starting early beats saving more"

Make a 32 to 40 second motion-graphics explainer about compound interest, built entirely with code and rendered to an MP4. It will be posted on a personal-finance channel, so it has to look like professional motion design: confident typography, a consistent visual style, smooth and purposeful animation, and a clear story a viewer can follow with the sound off.

## Deliverable
- `out/explainer.mp4`: 1920x1080, 30 frames per second, H.264 video in an MP4 container, `yuv420p` pixel format, 32 to 40 seconds long. No audio track is needed.
- The source that produces it, in this folder, and a `README.md` that gives the one command that renders the video again from scratch.
- Use any code-based approach you like: Remotion, or an HTML/CSS/SVG/canvas animation captured frame by frame with Playwright and encoded with ffmpeg, or anything similar. You may install npm packages.
- Everything on screen is generated by your code. No stock footage, photos, downloaded images or icon packs. Fonts must be ones already on this machine or bundled with a package you install.

## Story (five scenes, in this order; you decide the exact timing, wording of small labels and all the visuals)
1. **Title.** "Why starting early beats saving more", with "Compound interest in 35 seconds" as a smaller line.
2. **Meet Ana and Ben.** Ana invests $200 a month from age 25. Ben invests $400 a month from age 40. Both stop at 65 and both earn 7% a year.
3. **The race.** An animated chart of both balances growing year by year from age 25 to 65. Ben starts later and climbs steeply, but never catches Ana. Running totals count up as the chart draws.
4. **The result at 65.** Ana paid in $96,000 and ends with about $525,000. Ben paid in $120,000 and ends with about $324,000. Make the gap, and the fact that Ana paid in less, impossible to miss.
5. **Takeaway.** "Time does the heavy lifting. Start early." as a closing card.

## Requirements
- Every scene is animated: elements enter, move and leave with easing, and scenes are joined by transitions, not hard cuts to a static slide. Nothing sits completely still for more than about two seconds.
- All text is readable at 1080p on a phone: nothing smaller than about 28 px, good contrast, nothing cut off or overlapping, and each line stays on screen long enough to read.
- The numbers above are correct wherever they appear, and the chart's curves have the right shape (monthly deposits compounding at 7% a year).
- The first and the last frame are not blank.
