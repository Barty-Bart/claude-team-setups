# Bug reports from players (triaged, not yet investigated)

These came in through the feedback form after last week's release. Nobody on the team has reproduced them yet. Each one is a real problem for the person who reported it.

1. **"My best score got wiped."** "I finally got a really good run, something like 1,480 points. Next day the title screen says my best is 1. Before that my best was around 600 and that always stayed fine."

2. **"Coins vanish."** "A few times now my coin count went down in the middle of a wave, when I wasn't anywhere near the shop. It seems to happen when things get hectic and I'm mashing the keyboard."

3. **"Second run is weirdly easy."** "If I die late and press Play again, the new run feels much easier than when I freshly open the page. Enemies melt, and I move faster too, even though the shop says all my upgrades are back to level 0."

4. **"Steering cuts out on my phone."** "On my phone the character sometimes just stops, or lurches the wrong way, while my thumb is still down. I have to lift it and press again. It mostly happens when I hold the phone with both hands."

5. **"Slow motion."** "On my desktop the game is fine. On my old laptop, and on my phone when battery saver is on, everything moves in slow motion, maybe half speed. It isn't stuttering, it's just slow."

6. **"Invincible enemies."** "Late in a run some enemies, mostly the big ones, stop taking damage completely. My shots go straight through them and the wave can never be finished. It has happened three times, always in runs where I had bought a lot of upgrades."
